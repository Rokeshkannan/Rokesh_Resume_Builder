How it works

Features

Fresher and experienced paths

The form adapts: objective, internships and academic projects, or summary and work history.

Honest optimization

No invented skills, jobs or numbers. If a result has no figure, we ask for your real one.

Transparent ATS estimate

A 100-point score with an explanation for every part. It is an estimate, not an employer's ATS result.

Three ATS-safe templates

Privacy

Everything runs in your browser. Your details and job descriptions are never sent to a server or an AI service. Your draft is saved in this browser on this device only, and you can clear it at any time. Don't use a shared computer without clearing your draft.

Frequently asked questions

Is it really free?

Yes. No account, subscription or payment.

Does this guarantee I pass an ATS?

No. The score estimates how well your resume matches a job description using keyword and structure checks. Real systems differ.

Will it add skills I don't have?

Never. Missing keywords are only shown to you. You decide which ones are true for you.

Where is my data stored?

In your browser's local storage. Clearing browser data or using "Clear draft" removes it.
